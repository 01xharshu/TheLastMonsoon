## Research and design decisions
Suryagarh is a fictional north Indian district in 1857. These are dated category references, not authentication of each procedural building.
- [Delhi Tourism: Ghalib's haveli](https://delhitourism.gov.in/dt/unexplored_delhi/mirza_ghalib_ki_haveli.html): brick/column residential compound; use masonry, shaded courtyard and deep openings. Current restored museum detail is not proof of every 1857 fitting.
- [Ministry of Culture / West Zone Cultural Centre report](https://www.indiaculture.gov.in/sites/default/files/Annual_Reports_Organizations/Varshik_Report_2019_-_4729_03_06_2020.pdf): Bagore ki Haveli built by Mewar minister Amar Chand Badwa in 1751–1778; courtyard photo. [Rajasthan Tourism](https://www.tourism.rajasthan.gov.in/udaipur.html) confirms 18th-century origin. This is Rajasthan elite architecture, not a universal Awadh zamindar model; transfer only courtyard/compound principles provisionally. Do not copy late Shekhawati frescoes or royal palaces wholesale.
- [NCERT Colonialism and the Countryside](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/lehs301.pdf): landlords, ryots, jotedars, debt and revenue extraction differed by region. Model a fictional rent-collecting estate with grain storage/record desk/work yard and dependent cultivators, not a claim that all rich Indians owned enslaved workers. Begar/bonded/tenant labour need distinct story roles and specific regional evidence before dialogue/NPC labels.
- [Met Indian bronze lamp element, 18th–19th century](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/447421) and [Indian Museum lamp collection](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/lamp-traditions-in-india-indian-museum-kolkata/cwVxIvrDwDLuow?hl=en): oil/wick forms precede and overlap game date. Reuse existing `objects/oil_lamp.tscn` interaction, replace its placeholder visual locally with an original reservoir, wick, flame and support. Exact household lamp form remains provisional. [NGV oil lamp](https://www.ngv.vic.gov.au/explore/collection/work/57962/) is c.1880 and is expressly excluded as proof for 1857. Ritual museum lamps do not prove ordinary rural household ownership.
- [1856 Hindostan and Thibet road report catalogue](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/041-000133666) documents engineered roads in 1850–1855; [1855 Parliamentary public-works account](https://hansard.parliament.uk/Commons/1855-08-07/debates/c6ed2f67-5d87-4d89-8b50-e19d4b86ef2c/IndianFinance%E2%80%94EastIndiaCompanySRevenueAccounts) documents roads/canals. Village earth lanes remain fictional local tracks; distinguish them from trunk roads, avoid asphalt/painted markings and ubiquitous street lighting. Source existence alone does not establish surface detail.
- [Smithsonian Daniell, Old Tank, 1786](https://asia.si.edu/explore-art-culture/collections/search/edanmdm%3Afsg_S1999.8.3/) establishes tanks before 1857; prior [1833–34 Prinsep Raj Ghat](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/P_1871-0812-2526-2535) establishes stepped water access. Village pond/utility steps and short timber bridge are original fictional interpretations. Women collecting water/laundry, landless people and travellers gathering near night fires are proposed social activities, not sourced universal routines; final NPC clothing, seated contact and behavior approval remain required.
