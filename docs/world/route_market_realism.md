# Mountain road, village and market detail

Updated 2026-10-10. Status: integrated detail pass; native placement/render review PASS; final play/art and device performance review open.

`world/suryagarh/route_and_market_detail.gd` attaches through the real Suryagarh world startup. It uses the shared landscape survey and deterministic placement. The mountain fort trail has bounded dry-stone verge reinforcement, scattered shoulder stones and narrow uphill runoff beds. Stones are excluded within 5.4 m of nearby travelled roads, including switchback overlaps. The 571-stone assembly uses one instanced visual batch and one matching static collision body, with distance fading on the visual batch. Runoff beds are visual surface treatment, not excavated drainage or a water simulation.

Eight Bhairavpur stalls now have shaped sagging canvas with visible undersides and seams instead of rigid slab shades. Existing goods, counters, posts and hems are preserved. Reused editable/exported household assets add 64 baskets, grain sacks, mats, stools, buckets, crates and brass vessels across village stalls, six household work areas and six cantonment bazaar shops. Packed-earth work aprons follow the terrain beneath village work areas, with noisy faded edges to blend into the existing ground. No new human assets, terrain rebake, pond, HUD changes or modern street furniture.
