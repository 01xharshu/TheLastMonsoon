# Suryagarh Government House (1857 prototype)

The estate is a fictional district Government House in the central west-bank corridor, at the surveyed `GovernmentHouse` plot in `world/suryagarh/landscape_layout.gd`. Its front gate meets a carriage road from the existing main road; the north–south avenue reaches the portico. The same plot grades the terrain and clears generated vegetation, so later map expansion can preserve this location and access line.

The architectural precedent is [Calcutta's Government House, built 1799–1803](https://rajbhavankolkata.gov.in/html/background.html): the official history describes a three-storey central block with halls, radiating wings, formal gardens and gates. The game adapts those features to a smaller fictional residence with open reception, council/dining, and private floors. The plan, room dimensions, furniture, ironwork, and plantings are design inferences, **not** a reconstruction of a documented 1857 building or proof that a Governor-General resided in Suryagarh. The [New Delhi Viceroy's House was completed in 1929](https://www.rashtrapatibhavan.gov.in/making-rashtrapati-bhavan), so its architecture is not used as a period model. In 1857 story text, call this *Government House*; any later “Viceroy” designation needs a dated story context.
