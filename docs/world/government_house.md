# Suryagarh Government House (1857 prototype)

The estate is a fictional district Government House in the central west-bank corridor, at the surveyed `GovernmentHouse` plot in `world/suryagarh/landscape_layout.gd`. Its front gate meets a carriage road from the existing main road; the north–south avenue reaches the portico. The same plot grades the terrain and clears generated vegetation, so later map expansion can preserve this location and access line.

The architectural precedent is [Calcutta's Government House, built 1799–1803](https://rajbhavankolkata.gov.in/html/background.html): the official history describes a three-storey central block with halls, radiating wings, formal gardens and gates. The game adapts those features to a smaller fictional residence with open reception, council/dining, and private floors. The plan, room dimensions, furniture, ironwork, and plantings are design inferences, **not** a reconstruction of a documented 1857 building or proof that a Governor-General resided in Suryagarh. The [New Delhi Viceroy's House was completed in 1929](https://www.rashtrapatibhavan.gov.in/making-rashtrapati-bhavan), so its architecture is not used as a period model. In 1857 story text, call this *Government House*; any later “Viceroy” designation needs a dated story context.

Build authority: `world/suryagarh/settlements/government_house.gd`. The surveyed plot is centred at `(-390, -110)` with 192 × 184 m walled grounds; its terrain is graded to 8.5 m. The shared layout reserves the grounds from generated vegetation and connects its north gate to the main road. The built prototype has a three-storey central block, two wings, 74 glazed window openings (with two ground-floor passage openings replacing panes), a portico, garden beds and paths, furnished rooms, and two physical stair flights.

`tools/world/audit_civic_layout.gd`, `tools/world/validate_civic_world.gd`, `tools/world/validate_field_map.gd`, and the Forward+/Metal `tools/world/validate_government_house.gd` pass after terrain and map rebaking. The last test reports 65 clear gate-to-hall capsule samples, 46 stair samples, eight clear room/wing door samples, and solid support at the hall and both upper levels. [Estate](captures/24_government_house_exterior.png), [facade](captures/27_government_house_facade.png), [hall](captures/25_government_house_hall.png), and [upper floor](captures/26_government_house_upper.png) captures were inspected. These show a coherent large estate, but the interior furnishing, individual window hardware, plant variety, and materials are still prototype quality. The building currently uses 499 static collision bodies; profile it before adding more detail. An actual controlled player walk-through and production art/historical approval remain open.
