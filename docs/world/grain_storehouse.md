# Bhairavpur timber grain store

The existing grain store at world X -280, Z 285 now has a timber enclosure, thatched roof, open loading face, timber ramp and raised receiving platform. The existing market counter was moved to the side, and the bins to the rear, to retain a central passage. Outdoor sacks reuse the shared grain-sack scene. The form is a fictional interpretation for the game's Indian setting, inspired by the user's timber store reference; it is not a reconstruction of a specific documented Awadh building.

`CivicResidentPosts/GrainStorePorter/SupplyDelivery` reuses the existing MPFB street worker, bullock cart, work AnimationTree, hand solver, swept navigation, save state and simulation budget. In daylight, the worker starts when the player approaches within 50 m, lifts three sacks from the parked cart, carries them through the gate, places them on the raised platform and returns. This is a single local shipment: after the third sack, he rests; no repeated delivery or restocking cycle is claimed. The work state is saved through the existing `institution_operations` owner. The cart stays parked; the fixture does not demonstrate a road arrival.

Validation: `tools/world/validate_grain_storehouse.gd` checks a focused clear entrance and terrain-height sack placements. `tools/world/validate_grain_sack_delivery.gd` exercises a real-terrain, real-worker/cart fixture with the gate, a mid-carry restore and a 50 mm maximum measured hand target. `tools/world/validate_grain_delivery_world.gd` exercises the installed worker in the populated world and requires `TLM_GRAIN_SAVE_DIR` under the OS temporary directory; it checks a temporary on-disk save, three deliveries, clearance and platform support. Temporary projects, screenshots, logs and save data must be deleted after a run.

The populated-world headless test passed three deliveries, zero blocked ticks and 32.6 mm peak numerical hand-target error; it used a private whitespace-normalized copy of a concurrently edited player script. Focused Metal frames showed the store and worker, but the architecture remains visually simple and parts of the hand/carry motion were occluded. Continuous player-paced motion, clothing/contact, full-world rendering, exact historical construction and performance remain unapproved.
