# Fields and household cattle yard

Updated 2026-10-10. Status: integrated optimization baseline; final art and world FPS open.

Bhairavpur's twelve kitchen gardens and four mature estate plots now share a curved seven-leaf plant mesh and one wind material. Each plot uses a single plant MultiMesh and one textured, terrain-following soil mesh. Deterministic gaps, root offsets, leaf rotation, size and color variation break repeated planting. Soil ridges taper at the boundaries rather than sitting on raised rectangular slabs. The two estate plots used for sowing/hoeing retain the daily-activity owner's young crop treatment. Household garden relocation and field survey interactions remain active.

Plant detail ends at 100 m with a 15 m fade band; soil ends at 160 m. Small plant shadows are disabled. Shared world wind moves leaf tips on the GPU; there are no per-plant nodes, colliders, textures or script updates. This reduces submission and node costs but is not a measured whole-game FPS gain. The crop mesh is intentionally bounded; actual crop species and harvest stages need further authored art.

The existing House27 cattle shelter around (-321,289) batches its static surfaces into three material surfaces while preserving physical supports/collisions and independently changing fodder/water. A single 48-instance blade batch replaces 28 separate grazing boxes. The grazing patch remains outdoors beyond the shelter roof edge. Thatch has restrained procedural fiber/bundle shading with derivative filtering. Cow, caretaker, supplies, saved care state and contact routines are retained. The animal anatomy and shelter joinery remain art candidates.

Wild grass continues to use the shared building/plot exclusions in `grass_blades.gd`; cultivated plants remain bounded to designated plots. The focused validator checks House27's exclusion; this is not an exhaustive interior inspection of every building.
