# Abandoned roadside carts

Three wrecks sit on disused tracks along the far northern trunk and the outlying western lower service road. Sites stay at least 120 m from settlement plots and 180 m from named landmarks, and outside coach and consignment routes. The collapsed beds, snapped drawshafts, loose wheels and scattered cargo make them discoverable from the road. One retains part of a broken canvas hood. The wrecks sit on one side of the roadbed at road height. Terrain support and obstacle probes leave a clear strip for travel.

Hold the usual interact button beside the cargo box. Searching transfers one random cache of coins, food, bandages or trade goods and opens the lid. The save records the opened box under its stable world path alongside the inventory, so Continue restores the open lid without offering another payout.

Brass fittings and cotton bundles appear in the satchel. The merchant at the western Merchant Household buys one fitting for 5 rupees or one bundle for 3 rupees during his ordinary 09:00–17:00 business hours. Use the secondary interaction while carrying salvage. The existing mango order uses the primary interaction.

Run `python3 tools/world/run_npc_activities.py --abandoned-carts` to exercise actual world startup, road clearance, loot, merchant exchange and a fresh disk Continue. Add `--native --review-seconds 60` for temporary rendered views. The runner cleans captures, logs and disposable saves.
