The playable Bhairavpur stable is at world coordinates (-260, 194). It contains one bay horse, a timber and earth shelter with thatch roof, feed and water troughs, and plain leather tack. Arjun can take the horse with F, ride with W/S, steer with A/D, gallop with Shift, jump with Space, and dismount with F. The horse's `stolen` state becomes true when taken.

The material and tack choices are restrained to avoid modern equipment. Horse supply and cavalry use in 1857 are supported by [British Library India Office stud records](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/?f%5Bparent_reference_ssi%5D%5B%5D=IOR%2FL%2FMIL%2F5%2F459-467&f%5Burl_non_blank_si%5D%5B%5D=No&sort=hierarchy), [the July 1857 parliamentary discussion of cavalry horses](https://api.parliament.uk/historic-hansard/lords/1857/jul/13/the-mutiny-in-india-question), and an [1859 sketch of an Indian cavalryman](https://collection.nam.ac.uk/detail.php?acc=2002-04-909-18) in the National Army Museum collection. These establish the era context; they do not verify this particular stable architecture, horse breed, or exact saddle pattern.

The revised mesh narrows the barrel and defines the withers, croup, neck and muzzle more clearly; visible upper-leg muscles, knee/hock, cannon, pastern and hoof sections were also revised. [University of Missouri Extension horse conformation guidance](https://extension.missouri.edu/publications/g2837) was used for leg landmark checks. This is original procedural modeling, not a documented historical breed study or a veterinary-grade anatomical model.

Validation: `tools/world/validate_horse_stable.gd` checks spawn, take/theft flag, measured hand/rein and foot/stirrup alignment, riding out of the stable entrance, jump, dismount, and earth/road/timber ground selection in the loaded world. `tools/horses/capture_horse_review.gd` generated Forward+/Metal standing, walking, jumping, stamina HUD and gallop frames: `docs/world/captures/19_horse_rider_side.png`, `21_horse_walk.png`, `22_horse_jump.png`, `23_horse_stamina.png`, and `24_horse_gallop.png`. The horse has two-segment legs, walk and faster gait phases, an independent 240-point horse stamina reserve (versus Arjun’s 100), 6.0 m/s normal riding and 13.5 m/s gallop tuning, reins, stirrups, and saddle front/back. The stable has rafters, roof courses, and open feed/water troughs. The current riding pose bends Arjun's knees forward, keeps boots near the stirrup treads, lowers both hands toward the reins, and adds a small pace/airborne lean. The tack now has a rounded saddle profile and visible side/under girth. The gait now makes a small per-leg height correction to keep planted hooves near the ground and lift advancing hooves; the focused ride check bounds their measured clearance. Fresh Forward+/Metal side, walk, and jump captures were regenerated after this change. The horse still looks stiff in the walk and gallop frames, with visibly separate leg sections; body proportions, leg articulation, hoof grounding, jump arc, tail, tack construction, and rider contact through full motion remain simplified. The 13.5 m/s gallop is a short-burst gameplay setting, not a claim about a specific historical horse. The horse reserve drains during gallop and recovers separately; exhaustion prevents gallop until a quarter reserve has recovered. A distinct HORSE STAMINA bar appears above Arjun’s survival meters only while mounted. Hand/foot bone positions near tack anchors are a contact measurement, not proof that palms grip reins or boots bear weight in stirrups throughout the gait. Theft marks the horse and displays a message; no witness, pursuit, ownership persistence, or crime response is implemented.
