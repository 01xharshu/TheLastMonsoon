## Historical basis and retained limits

[NCERT, Ruling the Countryside, pp33–34](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/hess203.pdf) describes the upper steeping/lower beating arrangement, paddles, settling, pressing and drying, and reproduces William Simpson's1863 factory view. The green plant/blue processed dye distinction follows that process. The1863 image is within the user's permitted approximate date flexibility; this fictional estate is not a replica of a documented1857 site.

[John L. Hayes, Notes Upon Indigo,1873](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/62008/pg62008-images.html) is a later procedural comparison for perforated press boxes, cloth and shaded/slatted dry houses. Its date is beyond the approximate ten-year visual-reference range and is retained explicitly; it does not prove every detail existed here in1857. Exact press form, seasonal crop morphology, all work motions, final weathering, heating equipment, extended drainage and historical art remain open. No steam engine, electricity or assembly line is claimed. All architectural/crop additions are original code geometry; existing workman and Poly Haven crate provenance remains in the project.
