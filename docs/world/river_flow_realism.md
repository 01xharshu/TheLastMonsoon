# River flow and surface — 2026-10-07

The existing landscape layout remains the authority for river centre, width, banks, water level, swimming and the port/estuary. No terrain or bridge rebake was performed. `river_dynamics.gd` installs 18 water strips (51,876 vertices), clips the old surface only inside the playable reach, and preserves its distant sea continuation and the port ship's dry-hull exclusion.

Downstream follows increasing Z toward the port; upstream is decreasing Z. Current follows the analytic tangent of the existing river bends. Mid-channel speed varies from 0.85 m/s in the upper reach to 0.32 m/s near the estuary and softens near the bank. This is a gameplay approximation, not a surveyed Hooghly hydrodynamic model. Swim motion is relative to this current, including while the map/inventory is open. Occupied boats drift; unoccupied boats retain the existing moored behavior. Boat draft/bank collision and rider attachment remain active.

The surface has two low waves below 10 cm combined height, irregular advected normal ripples, silt-tinted shallow/deep water, restrained shoreline foam, sky lighting/reflection and a directional boat wake. The small boat's interior and the moored port ship are excluded from the water shader. Boat height follows a restrained portion of the surface heave. This does not add fluid simulation, breaking rapids, dynamic tides, underwater acoustics or screen-space reflection.

River audio has a continuous current layer and a separately phased close bank wash. The bank wash follows the terrain/water intersection by bounded sampling. Upstream water is slightly louder/higher pitched than the wider estuary. Both layers retire inland or without a valid world listener and retain shelter damping. They reuse the existing licensed field recording; this is not a newly sourced or locally authenticated upstream recording. Credits remain in `AUDIO_CREDITS.md`.
