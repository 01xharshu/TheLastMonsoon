# Ambient mix and Settings submenus — 2026-10-09

User rejected the constant buzzing sound and the long settings scroll. The continuous synthetic wind was the likely contributor: its short, low-frequency-heavy source and compressed import bypassed `audio_edges.gd`'s PCM loop crossfade. It is now a quieter 24-second filtered air texture with low rumble removed, imported as 16-bit PCM and crossfaded into a real loop. Source RMS falls from approximately -20.1 to -27.4 dBFS; wind playback gain falls from .45 to .12. A source-spectrum check found no dominant narrow tone (strongest spectral bin 0.27% of total power, negligible DC). This analysis is not subjective listening acceptance.

`Ambient` is a separate bus beneath Master. Wind, river/current and shoreline wash, fire, wildlife and environmental activity cues use it; gameplay effects restore Master when a pooled voice is reused. Ambient defaults to 40%, saves alongside existing settings and applies immediately; 0% mutes only the ambient bus. Music remains independently controlled. Fire and river also import as PCM so their existing loop crossfades actually run. Opening quiet/fade and outdoor shelter/location behavior are preserved.

Settings now starts with Audio, Camera, Controller, Display, Graphics and Keyboard controls. Each opens its own short submenu. Keyboard controls splits into Movement and Actions. Back/Escape/controller cancel goes to the containing category, and cancels an active key capture before navigating. Title and pause use the same panel. Existing settings and bindings are retained; camera angles now display degrees correctly. Device callbacks are disconnected on page changes. Small viewports may still scroll within a category; the combined all-settings scroll is removed.

Isolated wind/environment regressions and title/pause-menu integration PASS. Pages fit the pause panel's 500 px content budget.
