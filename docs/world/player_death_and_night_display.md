# Player death and night display

Implemented 2026-10-12. Health at zero opens a paused death screen with exactly **Load from last checkpoint** and **Back to Main Menu**. Escape/controller pause cannot resume a dead player. The load action rebuilds the production world through the existing save restore contract.

On foot, land impacts at 15.3 m/s or more are fatal (approximately a 12 m gravity drop). Lower impacts above 9 m/s retain graded damage. The shared movement wrapper covers normal movement, open inventory and dodge movement. Ridden horses and other mounted vehicles also track their highest position until landing; a continuous drop of 12 m onto supported land is fatal. Carts now continue off genuinely unsupported edges and fall under gravity; their swept landing ray triggers the same fatal impact. Horizontal fall motion also sweeps all existing cart clearance volumes to prevent tunnelling through walls. This is a baseline fall, not cart overturning, broken wheels or a bespoke death animation.

River exception: a ridden horse or horse cart entering deep river water kills its horse(s), stops traction/travel and releases Arjun's seat authority. His original collision layers return; a capsule-clear destination and swept path are selected in deep water. Carriage passengers prefer their own side rather than crossing the cabin. The jump starts at the seat, opens the relevant carriage door, temporarily ignores only that vehicle's colliders for 0.6 seconds, and then restores those collisions and normal swimming. There is no sideways teleport. If every candidate is blocked, the horses still die and the living rider remains attached while the exit retries at 0.25-second intervals. Saving is rejected during this brief escape. Bridges above water and shallow banks do not trigger this rule. Existing horse vitality/corpse/save systems retain the animal's death. Other vehicles retain the general fatal-land-fall rule; boats are not treated as horses.

Automatic checkpoints use a separate fourth internal file; the three manual slots remain available. Save restoration starts a checkpoint from the selected manual save, New Journey clears the prior journey checkpoint, and manual saves also refresh it. During ordinary grounded gameplay, a checkpoint is taken at the first permitted opportunity and then every 60 seconds. Existing pursuit/custody/cinematic/inquiry restrictions apply, and zero-health saves are rejected. Before the first checkpoint, the death button falls back to an available manual save and is disabled if none exists. Opening cinematics are not checkpointed.

Settings → Display, shared by title and pause, has a live 3D preview of the existing complete MPFB Arjun on an isolated empty city-road scene, with idle animation and no NPCs. Its Night brightness slider saves immediately. The preview and production Sun use the same ambient response: 0% = .025, 50% = the original .10, 100% = .70. Daytime ambient, moon timing and the world clock are preserved. A paused night responds to the option on resume; the preview responds while settings are open. The street is a small dedicated scene rather than a second production world, so settings does not spawn city traffic/population.
