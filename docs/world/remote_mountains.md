# Remote northeastern mountains

The dedicated mountain corridor starts on the existing fort trail near **(460, -250)** and runs through the eastern ridge, a high pass and the northeastern summit to a remote northern refuge. The terrain, settlement roads, port chase and saved landscape retain their existing authority. The region adds roughly **1.48 km** of terrain-draped trail, five discoverable map destinations, cairns, reused rocky outcrops, a crag chain and a roofed timber/stone refuge with a solid approach ramp. It contains no new settlement population or automatic combat spawns.

Open the field map and select **Mountain trailhead**, **High ridge lookout**, **Remote mountain pass**, **Northeastern summit** or **Northern mountain refuge**. Mountain pins use the existing nature icon; the refuge uses the shelter icon. Follow the existing fort approach to the trailhead, then the narrow worn earth-and-stone trail and cairns. The refuge entrance remains open and physically accessible.

`world/suryagarh/remote_mountains.gd` installs once during normal world startup after the existing terrain and nature setup. It uses the real terrain support collision for path/landmark anchoring and shared imported boulder geometry. Trail meshes are chunked with distance visibility; scenery and collision remain static, with no new per-frame autonomous callbacks. The route and field-map overlay share `remote_mountain_route.gd`. `tools/world/build_remote_mountain_route.gd` rebuilds its deterministic terrain survey, including the two saved solid-object exclusions and the retained forest-shrine/fort avoidance corridor found during physical validation. It does not rebake or replace the shared landscape.
