# Witnessed theft and sight-based pursuit

Updated 2026-10-09 17:24 IST. Integrated in the main Documents/GitHub project and worktree 9746. The main project's current UI remains authoritative for visual review; worktree UI may be older.

Successful first horse mounting and first cart driver boarding report horse_theft/cart_theft to CombatEncounters. Passenger boarding does not report theft. Explicit player_owned vehicles are exempt. A conscious nearby combat actor must see Arjun at the taking moment: perception uses range, forward field of view, stance/crop concealment and physics occlusion. No witness means no evidence and no wanted increase. An unattended theft is not retroactively reported when Arjun later passes an NPC. Vehicle first-take flags prevent remounting the same acquired vehicle from repeatedly adding stars.

A witnessed theft adds five points (one star); five stars is the cap. An officer who sees wanted Arjun interrupts normal duties and pursues. Officers remember their own last-seen position. After sight is lost they search that position rather than Arjun's live hidden location. Nearby police who cannot see him do not acquire him. Existing district/mounted patrol behavior is retained in the worktree; main's newer station custody, cinematic transfers and smoke behavior are retained. Station reports and approach pursuit now obey the same sight rules. Temporary recruited officers recover their previous movement mode and home after the wanted level clears.

Being inside a house or prone is not an automatic exemption: walls/roof edges/cover must actually block sight. Each unseen interval removes one star after 15 + 8 × current stars seconds (23 seconds at one star, 55 at five). Seeing Arjun resets that interval. Arrested/escorted Arjun cannot clear stars by being counted as hidden. Killing a recognized/engaged police officer adds ten points (two stars), capped at five. Damage reports are captured synchronously before lethal damage disables the victim. Crime score, evidence and last-known position persist through SaveManager.
