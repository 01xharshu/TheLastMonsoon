## 2. Proposed chapter: Beyond the hideout

After the rifle delivery and the subsequent [port consignment chase](rebellion_sequence.md), Mangal asks Arjun to learn who needs help and where the authorities are applying pressure. Arjun can explore, earn money, visit people, continue looking for Dev and choose local missions. Main missions do not automatically trigger at the end of every conversation. Side missions can be discovered during earlier exploration where existing story controls allow it; the full ally/factory arc unlocks after delivery.

Arjun gradually moves from personal revenge to protecting people. Villagers and workers already have their own ideas and resistance; he becomes a trusted participant. His growing reputation gives him contacts and assistance, never universal immunity or an automatic army.

## 5. Rebels living in the world

Groups inhabit selected shelters, river stops and village edges. Members rest, talk, maintain weapons and travel on authored routes. Initially prototype one group with three members; expand only after measured native cost and moving combat/contact review. This is a proposed cap, not a performance result.

When a trusted group witnesses Arjun being attacked by a recognised hostile party, available members can help. They must have actual sight or a separately implemented audible incident, a reachable route and a reason to intervene. Seeing Arjun assault a civilian must not recruit them. Unacquainted groups may defend a threatened worker without immediately becoming Arjun's followers.

Suggested lifecycle: routine → assess incident → approach → defend → regroup → return. Injured members retreat when possible. Members cannot fight while downed, detained, occupied by another mission or otherwise unavailable. Groups do not teleport, detect combat across the map or join through walls. A starting prototype limits assistance to one group and three responders; additional groups may remain in routine until slots are free. Repeated incidents use cooldowns and do not produce repeated trust rewards.

## 6. A working district

Plant fields are green; blue is associated with processed dye. Daily activity changes with work phase, daylight and mission outcomes. Retain open agricultural land and safe access paths; density should follow purpose.

The factory is a period dye-processing concern, with vats, work yards and stores. Exact chimney, steam machinery, architectural form and local crop calendar need supporting references before asset construction. Avoid a modern assembly-line factory. Reuse existing MPFB humans with complete bodies/foundations; work, carry, combat and injured motion require AnimationTree integration and contact review.
