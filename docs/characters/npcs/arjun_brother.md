# Dev — Arjun's elder brother and sepoy

Status: **RIGGED IDLE/WALK STUDY; COSTUME AND TRAVEL CONTACT OPEN** (2026-09-30).

The root project vision names Arjun's elder brother **Dev**. He serves as a sepoy, later disappears, and Arjun's guiding goal is **“Find Dev.”** His age is provisionally 29 while Arjun's reference age is 22. The relationship should read through a similar warm complexion, dark hair and broadly related facial structure. His stronger build, older face, shorter hair, uniform and steadier bearing distinguish him from Arjun. His regiment, exact service timeline, allegiance arc, location and fate are not fixed here.

## Walk and garment repair — 2026-09-30

The first stride separated the trousers from the boots because each entire trouser was weighted to its thigh. The rebuilt trousers now have eight sections following Dev's actual thigh/calf line, with a smooth knee blend from 0.43 to 0.56 m. Boots overlap the lower trouser and shoes align with the rig's foot centers. Fresh Metal [quarter-stride](dev_walk_25.png) and [opposite stride](dev_walk_75.png) show continuous coverage after the earlier detached/jagged hems were repaired.

Each authored frame adjusts the Root bone vertically from the lowest evaluated shoe vertex. This brings one sole to the flat studio floor; it does not lock a planted foot horizontally or handle slopes. `-- --walk --record` captures 72 frames at fixed 30 Hz through a dedicated 900×900 viewport. [The two-cycle studio video](dev_walk_studio.mp4) is 2.4 seconds at 30 fps. Six sampled frames across the cycle were visually inspected: knee/boot coverage remains connected, but the coat skirt stays stiff, hands are open, buttons/trim retain a small forward offset, and the gait is mechanical. This is an in-place motion study, with no world travel, navigation or narrative behavior. Normal-speed human approval and foot drift during actual travel remain open.

The [British Museum's East India Company uniform plates](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/P_1938-0212-11-1-37) describe two sepoys of the 65th Bengal Infantry in red full dress with yellow facings. The [National Army Museum's Company army overview](https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/armies-east-india-company) documents sepoys of the Bengal Army in 1857. These support a provisional red-coat direction, **not** the exact cut, colors or insignia for Dev before his unit is set. A [National Army Museum sepoy's turban or shako](https://collection.nam.ac.uk/detail.php?acc=1956-02-858-1) belongs to Madras Native Infantry around 1840 and should not be copied onto a Bengal character by assumption.
