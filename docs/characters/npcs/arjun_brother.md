This is an in-place motion study, with no world travel, navigation or narrative behavior. Normal-speed human approval and foot drift during actual travel remain open.

The [British Museum's East India Company uniform plates](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/P_1938-0212-11-1-37) describe two sepoys of the 65th Bengal Infantry in red full dress with yellow facings. The [National Army Museum's Company army overview](https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/armies-east-india-company) documents sepoys of the Bengal Army in 1857. These support a provisional red-coat direction, **not** the exact cut, colors or insignia for Dev before his unit is set. A [National Army Museum sepoy's turban or shako](https://collection.nam.ac.uk/detail.php?acc=1956-02-858-1) belongs to Madras Native Infantry around 1840 and should not be copied onto a Bengal character by assumption.
