# Bhairavpur Indian resident pair — first visual candidate

Status: **IN_WORLD_PREVIEW / VISUAL_REVIEW_FAILED / MOTION_BLOCKED** (2026-09-24). The two figures are visible in the Bhairavpur lane at `(-310, 7.2, 230)`. They are deliberately a small pair, not a crowd or an approved population set.

The male and female adult basemeshes and editable 53-bone game-engine rigs were created with the installed MakeHuman/MPFB extension. Their source `.blend` files and SHA-256 manifests are in `WorkingAssets/NPCs/village_farmer/` and `WorkingAssets/NPCs/village_woman/`; the common reproducible builder is `tools/characters/build_village_farmer.py` (`-- --female` for the woman). The Godot `.glb` exports are baked static previews, 4.42 and 5.31 MB. Neither contains a skin or animation. The source rigs are preserved for fitted costume and motion work. No rejected Arjun body, face, clothing, or texture was used.

## Period dress basis

Suryagarh's 1857 setting is fictional and closest visually to the northern Indian village/Patna corridor. For the man, the garment types are a cotton tunic/top, wrapped dhoti and cloth head wrap. A [Patna-region mid-18th to mid-19th-century weaver painting](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/A_1989-1225-0-18) depicts a standing figure in a white dhoti and top, and a [19th-century Varanasi painting](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/A_1957-1012-0-1-11) depicts a dhoti and turban. For the woman, a [Patna fruit seller painting dated 1800–1850](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/A_1989-1225-0-6) shows a sari covering the head over a blouse. The model uses a cotton sari and blouse. These are **garment references**, not proof that all Indian peasants wore a single uniform, or that this particular drape is final. The museum paintings describe traders/figures, not these characters' exact occupation or identity.

Only bundled MPFB core assets were used for body, rig, eyes, hair, skin maps, and the fitted upper-garment topology; the dhoti, sari drape, head cloth and material colors are original. The male complexion uses the MPFB `middleage_african_male` texture on an independently shaped body; the female uses a tinted MPFB `middleage_asian_female` map. These are complexion choices, not identities or ancestry labels. [MakeHuman Community](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/about/license.html) and the [MPFB license](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/blob/master/LICENSE.md) identify core assets as CC0, with MPFB program code under GPL. The builder calls the installed extension; no extension source code is bundled in the game.

## Evidence and remaining work

`indian_peasant_pair_world.png` is a fresh 2026-09-24 **Godot 4.7.2 Forward+/Metal** render, after scene import. Blender front/profile renders live next to each `.blend`. Structural import and world loading passed. The fitted upper garment eliminates the earlier cylindrical sleeves, and correcting the automatic MPFB body mask restored attached hands and full lower legs. The world render still does **not** pass visual approval: the fitted top retains modern seams/collar, the male head wrap is cap-like, the dhoti needs credible tucking/pleats, and the woman's sari pallu is still a stiff strip. The pair has no collision, speech, schedule, or interaction. Do not multiply these across the map until cloth and motion are reviewed in-world. The combined static exports total 9,726,576 bytes; a local 16GB M4 Metal capture peaked at 733,265,920 bytes resident memory. Actual 8GB-device performance has not been measured.

`tools/characters/export_village_motion_candidate.py` makes separate rigged `idle`/`walk` studies and SHA manifests in both source folders. The first export visibly broke through the clothing because glTF did not preserve the MPFB body and garment masks. The revised exporter bakes masked rest-pose meshes while retaining skin weights; both clips now load in Godot, and the earlier body/trouser breakthrough is absent from the sampled renders. `tools/characters/capture_indian_motion_candidate.gd -- --male-only` and `--female-only` captured four phases each on Godot 4.7.2 Forward+/Metal: `indian_peasant_{male,female}_walk_{00,25,50,75}.png`. These are paused pose samples, not a normal-speed playthrough. The man’s dhoti and head cloth and the woman’s sari/pallu remain stiff; foot contact, full-cycle deformation, and period tailoring are not approved. **MOTION_BLOCKED**: the rigged studies remain in `WorkingAssets/` and are not used by the playable pair. A successful glTF import or animation-name check does not certify motion/contact.

Next: remodel the period seams, turban and true sari/dhoti drapes; improve cloth rigging and inspect full idle/walk cycles and foot contact at normal speed in Metal before replacing the static previews. Only then add residents or NPC behavior.

## Independent AnimationTree studies — 2026-09-27

`characters/npcs/indian/indian_npc_candidate.gd` loads either rigged study with its own AnimationPlayer and BlendTree: idle and walk feed a Blend2, with TimeScale on the walk branch. Both clips loop; start/stop blending takes 0.2 seconds. Playback rate is adjustable, but has not been calibrated to ground travel. These are in-place studies, with no navigation or foot locking, and the playable static pair remains unchanged.

`tools/characters/validate_indian_animation_tree.gd` passes on Godot 4.7.2: two distinct trees/graphs, thigh motion, intermediate/full blend, multiple loops, idle recovery and independent stop. Evidence: `animation_tree_validation.json`. This is functional evidence only.

The existing Metal capture tool accepts `-- --tree` to run the trees continuously and stop the first actor independently in the last sample. Tree screenshots use `indian_peasant_pair_tree_*.png`; their suffixes are sample identifiers, not exact walk phases. Initial pixels showed rigid dhoti/sari silhouettes and a female sleeve/upper-arm gap. Cloth, tailoring and sole contact remain unapproved; tree readiness does not clear the visual rejection.

### Garment deformation repair — 2026-09-27

The motion exporter now restores female arm skin using deform-bone weights instead of the earlier coordinate-only mask. Fresh Metal pixels show the sleeve opening filled. Lower drapes and borders retain an anchored waist with smooth thigh influence reaching 30% at the hem, replacing the all-pelvis weighting. Both candidate `.blend`/`.glb` files and SHA manifests were regenerated; the original MPFB sources and playable static exports were preserved. Tree validation passes again. These weights are a limited deformation improvement, not cloth simulation or period tailoring approval.

`-- --tree --record` produces a lightweight Metal studio sequence: 120 frames evaluated at a fixed 30 Hz, three seconds walking followed by one second with the farmer stopped and the woman continuing. `indian_peasant_tree_motion.mp4` plays those frames at 30 fps. This demonstrates candidate pose deformation and independent stop, not real-time gameplay performance or terrain contact. Full-world recording stalled after initial samples; the studio avoids that unresolved capture limitation.

Studio frame 30 exposes male knee breakthrough at the dhoti hem and visibly floating feet; the woman also retains a light patch through the blouse. **Motion/visual review still fails.** Next: fit garment clearance across the whole cycle, repair the remaining upper-body mask, then author a grounded gait and calibrate travel/cadence before playable integration.
