# Additional Indian NPC candidates — 2026-09-28

Two independent adult candidates were authored beyond the existing farmer/woman pair: a fruit seller with an indigo sari and head basket, and a weaving assistant in a white top/dhoti with blue head cloth and folded fabric. Their body proportions and costume colors differ from the first pair. These are study roles and visual designs, not claims about all Indian residents.

The period direction uses the British Museum's [1800–1850 Patna fruit-seller painting](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/A_1989-1225-0-6) and [1800–1850 Patna weaver-and-assistant painting](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/A_1989-1225-0-18). The references depict a head-carried basket and a standing assistant holding fabric. Our procedural geometry is an interpretation, not a replica or approved costume. Both objects are Company School works, so details warrant independent review before final art approval.

Reproducible builds: `tools/characters/build_village_farmer.py -- --fruit-seller` and `--weaver-assistant`, followed by matching roles in `tools/characters/export_village_motion_candidate.py`. Editable MPFB sources, rigged motion studies, front/profile renders and SHA manifests are in `WorkingAssets/NPCs/village_fruit_seller/` and `WorkingAssets/NPCs/village_weaver_assistant/`. Static exports are under `characters/npcs/`; the motion GLBs remain separate candidates. All four Indian candidates now have their own idle/walk AnimationTree; `animation_tree_validation.json` passes four distinct trees, looping, pose motion, blending and independent stopping on Godot 4.7.2.
