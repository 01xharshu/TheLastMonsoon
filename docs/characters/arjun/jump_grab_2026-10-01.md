# Wall jump, grip and deliberate ascent — 2026-10-01

The owner requested a physical jump and catch before climbing, with tall walls retaining their role as security barriers. The authored tall-wall route now requires a new jump, a reachable hold and repeated deliberate moves. Pressing jump while standing no longer launches a complete wall ascent.

## Playing the route

- Face the projecting stones and press **Space** (controller **cross**) to jump. Arjun must actually leave the ground and rise at least 0.35 m before catching. The catch window lasts 1.2 seconds and the wall must be within 0.90 m.
- He settles into a two-handed hang over 0.32 seconds and waits. The AnimationTree has a distinct hang pose between reach and pull.
- Press jump again to pull his body 0.35 m beneath the held stone. Further presses perform one hand reach, opposite boot placement and supported upward push at a time. Holding the button does not repeat moves.
- At the coping, another press commits the crouched mantle and standing recovery, after collision sweeps and landing support checks.
- **S / backward stick** lets go and moves away from the wall before restoring the standing capsule. Existing gravity resumes. Climbing and hanging consume 2.5 stamina per second; empty stamina releases the grip. Stamina does not regenerate while climbing.
- A small control prompt appears beside Arjun only while waiting on the wall.

## Security and route constraints

The wall route uses its authored projecting masonry, with the default usable lane narrowed from 2.3 m to 0.45 m on either side of its centre. That keeps the character and hand targets inside the visible stone strip. Blank masonry beside it cannot start the tall climb. Smooth tall walls, holds above jumping reach, excessive horizontal distance and protected surfaces reject entry. Low objects, surveyed rock ledges and registered windows retain their existing traversal profiles.

Authoring metadata: `climb_blocked=true` denies wall/ordinary ledge entry; `climb_lane_half_width` controls the route lane; `climb_min_hand_row` defaults to 3; `climb_hold_missing_rows` denies a catch or advance onto a missing hand/boot row. The coping must remain within reach of the last authored handhold. Failed advance requests leave the current move boundary unchanged. Collision masks remain active; travel uses the existing solid capsule sweeps and animated torso/head clearance. A blocked movement rolls back its pose phase along with travel progress.

The current compound still has one authored weak point with continuous projecting stones. Other tall wall faces remain barriers. This pass does not redesign the compound, add guard responses, or validate NPC navigation security.
