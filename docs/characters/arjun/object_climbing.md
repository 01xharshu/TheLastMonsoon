# Object-dependent climbing — 2026-10-01

Solid-body follow-up: ledges and windows now share collision-checked travel; collision stays enabled and blocked routes are rejected. Crouched body clearance and standing restoration are verified, and solid ledges also constrain the animated torso/head outside the face. Current details and evidence: [solid climbing](solid_climbing_2026-10-01.md). Fresh low/high Metal samples and all eight world windows PASS after the change. Older motion previews below predate this collision repair.

Ordinary solid ledges now use measured surface height and support depth instead of the tall-wall step sequence. Below 1.05 m above the feet, Arjun uses a shorter supported step; higher ledges up to 2.15 m use a deeper crouched mantle. The duration varies from approximately 1.65 to 2.8 seconds with height. A low ray detects obstacles below chest level. The landing probe requires a horizontal surface far enough behind the edge to support the standing capsule; narrow unsupported lips are rejected.

The shared traversal pose lowers the body during hand support, lifts each boot before crossing, and transfers the palms from the rim onto the top as the hips advance. Windows retain their sill grip and lintel crouch, while authored walls with holds retain the alternating reach/boot/push sequence. Sloped static tops and separate broken stones now use local hand/landing surveys and surface-aligned contacts; see [rock climbing evidence and limits](rock_climbing.md). Ladders and moving objects require further work.
