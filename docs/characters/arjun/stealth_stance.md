# Arjun prone and cover — 2026-09-24

Z (controller D-pad down) toggles prone. Arjun lowers to a horizontal collider, crawls at 1.15 m/s, cannot sprint or jump, and the camera comes down with him. H takes cover only when a solid crate, tree, cart or similar object is directly in front of him. C (controller right-stick press) takes nearby solid cover or enters free crouch when no cover is available. Sprint with movement requests a clearance-checked return to standing from crouch/prone before running. Cover uses a lowered collider and a 2 m/s crouch. Pressing the same control leaves that stance when there is standing clearance; walking away releases cover. G stows or draws the selected weapon. Both stances are unavailable while swimming, climbing, mounted, in a menu, or in a held object interaction.

Holding aim in cover raises Arjun enough to look and shoot over low objects. Existing firearm rays still begin at the weapon muzzle and stop at intervening geometry, so a tree or tall cart body blocks a shot until the player moves to an exposed edge. Combat inputs remain available from cover. This is physical cover positioning and procedural body motion; patrol sight now uses the stance eye height, solid line of sight, and bounded crop concealment. Low sparse village gardens do not hide a standing or crouching person. See [crop contract](../../world/crop_concealment_contract.md).
