# Arjun prone and cover — 2026-09-24

Z (controller D-pad down) toggles prone. Arjun lowers to a horizontal collider, crawls at 1.15 m/s, cannot sprint or jump, and the camera comes down with him. C (controller right-stick press) takes cover only when a solid crate, tree, cart or similar object is directly in front of him. Cover uses a lowered collider and a 2 m/s crouch. Pressing the same control leaves that stance when there is standing clearance; walking away releases cover. Both stances are unavailable while swimming, climbing, mounted, in a menu, or in a held object interaction.

Holding aim in cover raises Arjun enough to look and shoot over low objects. Existing firearm rays still begin at the weapon muzzle and stop at intervening geometry, so a tree or tall cart body blocks a shot until the player moves to an exposed edge. Combat inputs remain available from cover. This is physical cover positioning and procedural body motion; enemy sight awareness and enemy attacks have not been integrated with it yet.

Implementation: `player/stealth_stance.gd`, `player/player_controller.gd`, `player/player.tscn`, `project.godot`. Focused world check `tools/world/validate_stealth_stance.gd` PASS for prone collision and recovery, crate/tree/cart cover detection, cover exit, and aim/attack availability.
