# Firearm scale and reload motion — 2026-09-25

The current Enfield GLB measures 1.410 m along the barrel axis. The currently wired, owner-rejected Arjun rig measures 1.733 m tall by mesh bounds, making the rifle 81.4% of that body's height. A [Smithsonian Pattern 1853 example](https://www.si.edu/object/1853-enfield-rifle-musket%3Anmah_1201578) measures 140.335 cm overall. The export is therefore close to an actual full-length rifle musket. This is a dimensional finding, **not** approval of how it looks against a replacement Arjun. The rifle was not shortened. The third-person long-gun aim camera was eased back from 0.75 m to 0.95 m and widened from 51° to 56° to reduce perspective magnification.

`player/arjun_equipment.gd` now drives a timed reload pose from each gun's existing reload timer. The Enfield/double gun rises muzzle-up; the right hand holds the stock while the left moves from fore-end to pouch to muzzle and back. The Enfield ramrod and tip extend and return during the sequence. The Adams pistol lowers toward the body while the left hand moves from pouch toward the cylinder. `player/first_person_view.gd` copies the long-gun and ramrod transforms so this motion is present in both camera modes. The existing ammunition count and firing gates are unchanged.
