# Audio credits and licences

The following recordings are retained in `WorkingAssets/Audio/world_sources`. Runtime derivatives are in `audio/ambience`; source URLs, SHA-256 checksums and processing changes are listed in the source manifest. These recording licences apply to those sound assets and their derivatives.

- **Fire Crackling** — AntumDeluge, [OpenGameArt source](https://opengameart.org/content/fire-crackling). [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Mono 22050 Hz conversion with filtering and loop preparation.
- **Mudchute cow 1** — Secretlondon, [Wikimedia Commons source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mudchute_cow_1.ogg). [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Mono 22050 Hz conversion with filtering/fades; this derivative is distributed under CC BY-SA 3.0. An Irish Moiled recording is used as a generic cattle candidate, not claimed as exact Indian cattle breed sound.
- **Rivernoise** — Secretlondon, [Wikimedia Commons source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rivernoise.ogg). [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Mono 22050 Hz conversion with filtering and loop preparation; this derivative is distributed under CC BY-SA 3.0. Recorded at River Esk, Scotland; used as a water texture rather than claimed as a recording of Suryagarh or Hooghly.
- **House Sparrow** — Gypsypkd, [Wikimedia Commons source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:House_Sparrow.ogg), public-domain dedication by its author. Recorded in India. Five-second excerpt, mono conversion, filtering and fades.

`audio/world` contains original deterministic synthesized contact sounds rebuilt by `tools/audio/build_world_effects.py` and `tools/audio/build_work_effects.py`. Existing firearm, horse, menu, music and other recordings retain their original provenance; this document does not replace their credits.

- **Different steps on wood, stone, leaves, gravel and mud** — TinyWorlds, edited from PDSounds recordings, [OpenGameArt source](https://opengameart.org/content/different-steps-on-wood-stone-leaves-gravel-and-mud), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Six active dirt/gravel, stone and timber variants converted to mono 22050 Hz PCM. Original ZIP and checksum retained in the source folder.
