# Generated dialogue voices

The British English dialogue and street voices were generated offline using
[Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), distributed under the
Apache License 2.0. The Indian English dialogue and street voices use
[Indic Parler-TTS](https://huggingface.co/ai4bharat/indic-parler-tts), also
published under the Apache License 2.0. The Indian model weights used for this batch came from the [public model mirror](https://huggingface.co/naklitechie/indic-parler-tts), with SHA-256 `c68daecb60f80c8f1faf0a6d2e6ddd6de8e224fb19750f3e9a33bca43c552c90`. The generation script is
`tools/voices/generate_inquiry_voices.py`.

Recorded lines are keyed by the SHA-256 of the speaker name, a newline, and
the exact dialogue text. This keeps the same character's voice stable and
prevents a changed subtitle from playing an outdated recording. The gameplay
uses the generated audio files and does not require either model at runtime.

The generator uses Kokoro `bm_george`, `bm_lewis`, `bm_daniel`, and
`bf_emma` for the British characters. Indic Parler speaker prompts use Raghav
for Arjun and Indian male residents, Ravi for Chacha, Kabir for Mangal,
Dinesh for the station officer and fighter, Vikas for the driver, Aakash for the witness,
Tarun for village protectors, and Meera for Indian female residents. These are model speaker labels, and each generated performance
still needs listening review.

Mocking laughter: `mocking_laugh.mp3`, “Hearty Man Laugh 02” from https://sfxmint.com/sounds/laugh-02, CC0 1.0; downloaded from https://sfxmint.com/dl/laugh-02.mp3. Listening and acting review pending.

Errand requests use `errand_medicine.wav`, `errand_money.wav`, and
`errand_food.wav`, generated with Meera, Ravi, and Dinesh respectively by
`tools/voices/generate_errand_voices.py`. The same script generates exact-text
Chacha lesson keys and named contextual conversation files. All are offline
recordings; generated performances and the in-world mix require listening review.

The selected primary female street profile uses the Indic Parler `Mary` voice
for its complete everyday set and four playful adult-resident exchanges;
`Sneha` remains a separate alternate profile. Arjun replies use `Raghav`.
The `context_mary_*` and `context_arjun_mary_flirt_*` WAVs were generated by
`tools/voices/generate_errand_voices.py`. They are candidates for listening and
mix review, not a claim of studio-production approval.
