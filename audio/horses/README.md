The horse uses spatial players with restrained levels: hoofbeats follow the gait only while grounded and select earth, packed road, or timber bridge foley from the ground beneath the horse, leather plays on mounting, dismounting, and takeoff, landing plays on actual ground contact, the first taking of the stable horse plays the neigh, and idle snorts are intermittent. The waveform and trigger checks are technical evidence; final in-game listening and mixing remain to be reviewed, against the current background music.
